# MetFix Courses

> Metabolic health education for coaches, affiliates, specialty populations, and individuals.

URL: https://metfix.org/classes
Alias: https://metfix.org/courses

## Pathway

- Start free: [What Is MetFix?](https://whatis.metfix.org/) (~1 hour)
- Specialty tracks: typically 6 to 40 hours depending on the course
- Essentials Advanced: online professional curriculum; required to apply for affiliation
- Foundations: two-day in-person seminar (see [affiliate-seminars.md](https://metfix.org/affiliate-seminars.md))

MetFix is education, not a certification body. Learners can move at their own pace online.

## Catalog themes

Offerings change; check the live catalog. Themes represented on the site include:

- What Is MetFix? (free intro; member and coach tracks)
- First responder metabolic health and occupational longevity
- Women's midlife / perimenopause metabolic health
- Individual metabolic literacy (labs, diet, talking with clinicians)
- Athletic Teen Performance
- Medical Nutrition Training for clinicians
- MetFix Essentials Advanced for professional coaches and affiliates

Affiliates typically receive online course access at no cost and can earn revenue share when clients enroll through them.
