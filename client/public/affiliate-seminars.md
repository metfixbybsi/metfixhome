# MetFix Foundations Seminars

> Two-day in-person metabolic health training for coaches and gym owners.

URL: https://metfix.org/affiliate-seminars
Events listing: https://brokenscience.org/events/

## What Foundations is

Foundations is the hands-on practical capstone of the MetFix curriculum. Coaches leave with the metabolic health coaching framework practiced and ready to use with clients.

It is strongly recommended for coaches and owners on the affiliate path. It can be completed after affiliation. MetFix Essentials Advanced (online) is required to apply for affiliation.

## Who attends

Coaches, gym owners, trainers, and practitioners. Medical professionals also attend. Affiliate gyms can apply to host a Foundations Seminar.
